About

I am a master's student in economics at Yonsei University. My research interests lie in urban economics, international trade, and industrial organization.

I am interested in how networks shape economic opportunities. My research examines firms' trading relationships and households' proximity to family, drawing on firm-to-firm transaction records, historical census records, and firm-level trade data.
